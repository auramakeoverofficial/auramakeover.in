How to Choose the Right Makeup Artist

Complete Guide for Brides

Check availability on WhatsApp

By the Aura Makeover team · Published 2 May 2026 · Updated 31 October 2026

Your makeup artist is with you on one of the biggest mornings of your life. Their skill matters, but so do their punctuality, their hygiene, and how calm they keep you. Here's what to check before you book.

1. Look at Real Work

Ask to see photos of real brides, ideally unedited or straight from the camera. Heavily filtered pictures tell you very little about how the makeup looks in person.

2. Make Sure Their Style Matches Yours

Some artists are known for bold glam, others for soft, natural looks. Scroll through their recent work. If nothing looks close to what you want, keep looking.

3. Read Reviews

Google reviews from real clients tell you what a portfolio can't: whether the artist arrived on time, how the makeup held up, and how they handled a stressful morning.

4. Ask About Products and Hygiene

Ask which brands they use and how they keep their kit clean. Sanitised brushes and disposable applicators should be standard.

5. Book a Trial if You Can

A trial lets you see the look on your own face, in daylight, before the wedding. It's also a good way to see how well the artist listens.

6. Understand the Price

Ask exactly what's included: hair, draping, travel and touch-ups. Quotes are much easier to compare once you know what each one covers. Our bridal makeup cost guide shows typical prices in Faridabad.

7. Check the Booking Terms

Find out how much advance is needed, whether it's refundable, and how travel is charged. A good artist will be upfront about all of this before you pay anything.

Final Thoughts
