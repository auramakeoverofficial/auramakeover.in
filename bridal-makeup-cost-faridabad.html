Bridal Makeup Cost in Faridabad (2026)

Complete Price Guide for Brides

By the Aura Makeover team · Published 2 May 2026 · Updated 31 October 2026

It's usually the first question brides ask us, and a fair one. Here's what makeup costs at Aura Makeover in 2026, what changes the price, and how to get the most from your budget.

Our 2026 Prices

  • Bridal makeup: ₹20,000 – ₹25,000
  • Engagement makeup: ₹7,000 – ₹10,000
  • Reception makeup: ₹15,000
  • Haldi or Mehndi look: ₹5,000 – ₹7,000
  • Party makeup: ₹3,000 – ₹5,000
  • Hair styling (add-on): ₹2,000 – ₹5,000

If you're booking all three main functions, the Bridal Full Package (engagement, wedding and reception) is ₹35,000 – ₹45,000. That's less than booking each one separately.

What Changes the Price

  • HD or airbrush. Airbrush usually costs a little more because of the equipment and technique, but it lasts longer.
  • Products. Premium brands cost more, and they're a big reason makeup lasts and photographs well. We use brands like Huda Beauty, NARS and Forever 52.
  • Hair and draping. Hair styling is an add-on, priced by how elaborate the style is.
  • Location. Travel to your venue is added on top, and so is accommodation for out-of-town weddings.
  • Experience. An experienced artist costs more. You're paying for someone who knows how to keep makeup intact through a long, emotional day.

HD vs Airbrush: Is the Extra Cost Worth It?

For a long summer wedding, or if your skin gets oily, usually yes. For a shorter day function, or if your skin is dry, HD often does the job just as well. Our HD vs airbrush guide goes into more detail.

How Booking Works

A 50% advance confirms your date. Once your date is held we turn other brides away, so the advance is non-refundable if you cancel. Message us with your dates and functions and we'll send you an exact quote.

Final Thoughts

Price matters, but it shouldn't be the only thing you compare. Look at real work, read reviews, and choose someone you'll trust on the morning of your wedding. Our guide on how to choose a makeup artist has a checklist.
